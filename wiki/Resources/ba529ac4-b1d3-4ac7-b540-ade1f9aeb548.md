# Area: Personal Asset Allocation Policy
Rules for capital deployment:

- Real Assets: 15% physical real estate / debt instruments.
- High-Growth / Venture: 15% concentrated technology and AI bets.
- Maintain 6 months of living expenses in high-yield liquid cash reserves.
