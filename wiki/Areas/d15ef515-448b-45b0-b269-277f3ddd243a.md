# Resource: Tiago Forte's P.A.R.A. Framework
Four distinct top-level categories based on actionability:
1. Projects: Short-term efforts with a specific goal and deadline (e.g., 'Launch SecondSelf MVP').
2. Areas: Long-term responsibilities with standards to maintain over time (e.g., 'Health', 'Architecture').
3. Resources: Topics or themes of ongoing interest for reference (e.g., 'Machine Learning', 'Recipes').
4. Archives: Inactive items from the other three categories preserved for retrieval.
